| What | Source |
|---|---|
| FY 2024-25 rates, slabs, 87A, surcharge, marginal relief, cess (AY 2025-26) | Income Tax Department, "Tax rates — Individual": https://incometaxindia.gov.in/Documents/Left%20Menu/TAX%20RATES_Indvl.htm and https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/tax-rates |
| 10-year rate history (cess 4% from AY 2023-24; AY 2025-26 new-regime table) | https://incometaxindia.gov.in/Charts%20%20Tables/Tax-rates-for-last-10-years/Individual-and-HUFs-Tax%20Rates.html |

Note on naming: the brief says "Finance Act 2024". There were two in 2024. The interim Finance Act, 2024
(February) did not change individual rates; the FY 2024-25 new-regime slabs and the Rs 75,000 standard
deduction come from the Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 (July Budget, assent 16 August 2024). The rule text cites
the latter.

Note on the Income-tax Act, 2025: it replaces the 1961 Act from tax year 2026-27. FY 2024-25 and FY 2025-26
are both governed by the 1961 Act, so all section numbers here are 1961-Act numbers.
